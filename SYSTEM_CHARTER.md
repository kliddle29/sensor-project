# Don't Leave

## It sees

| Reading | What it reads |
|---|---|
| `cm` | nobody: 169 (empty table) · no echo prints 400 · leaning in: 10, 12, 14 |
| `breath` | nobody: 4–7 (idle noise) · a light breath: about 25 · a hard breath: 30+ |

## It calls you

| Name | Rule | Hold |
|---|---|---|
| devoted | `breath` over 25 | 3 s |
| deserter | `cm` 50 to 150 | 2 s |
| nobody | `cm` over 150 | 5 s |

Between rules, it keeps the last name.

## It does

| Name | Action |
|---|---|
| devoted | blue LED steady, buzzer silent, the OLED prints DEVOTED |
| deserter | red LED flashes, a siren that speeds up, the OLED prints DESERTER |
| nobody | every light off, the screen blank but for one dot that blinks every 5 s: it waits |
